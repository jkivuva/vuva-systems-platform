Demonstration

The business problem

Freight businesses run on phone calls and paper. Customers cannot see where their cargo is, dispatch coordinates drivers over WhatsApp and management hears about delays from complaints.

What the system does

One platform handles bookings, dispatch, drivers, tracking, customer updates and payments. When something changes, everyone who needs to know finds out on their own.

Who uses it

Customers book and track their cargo. Drivers get their routes and capture proof of delivery. Dispatch assigns work and management sees revenue and problems as they happen.

AI Layer

Where the AI fits in the operation.

Assistive, supervised

ETA prediction

Arrival times update from route progress and past trip data, so the customer hears the same estimate the operations team works from.

Exception detection

Late shipments are flagged early. Dispatch sees what is affected and what to do about it before customers start calling.

Route recommendations

The system suggests the best stop order for each run. Drivers can change it with one tap and the system learns from those changes.

Demand forecasting

Volume forecasts per branch help plan staffing and where vehicles should be parked for the week ahead.

Driver performance analysis

On time rates, problems and customer ratings per driver, meant for coaching rather than monitoring.

AI customer support

Answers delivery questions on WhatsApp straight from live records, and passes complicated cases to staff with the full history attached.

The system logs everything the AI does. Customer messages use approved wording, and anything unclear goes to a person.
