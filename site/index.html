W.01LOGISTICS / FREIGHT

Limitless Logistics

A logistics platform we built for Limitless Logistics, a freight company. It covers the customer site, an AI assistant, quote requests, shipment tracking and an operations dashboard.

THE PROBLEM
Customers could not see where their freight was. Drivers, warehouse work and quotes lived in separate tools.
THE SYSTEM
Customer tracking portal, AI assistant for status questions, online quotes and one dashboard for the operations team.
THE SOLUTION
Every shipment is visible in one place. Customers check status themselves while dispatch manages drivers from a single screen.
STATUS
Delivered. The logistics demo shows the same pattern with sample data.

Blitz Restaurant

THE SYSTEM

Every order flows from payment to kitchen to rider automatically, and managers see a report for each branch.

W.05AI ENGINEERING / FULL-STACK

AI Content Experience Platform

A content platform I built to show how AI drafting, retrieval-augmented search and human review fit together in one editorial system.

THE PROBLEM
Teams want AI help writing and finding content, but most tools publish whatever the model produces with no record of who checked it.
THE SYSTEM
Next.js front end on a headless CMS abstraction, a RAG pipeline that cites its sources and refuses when coverage is thin, an evaluation harness, and a workflow engine where publishing requires a person’s approval. Everything is logged.
WHAT IT SHOWS
AI-assisted workflows, grounded answers with sources, human-in-the-loop review, governance you can inspect, accessibility and edge deployment. The demo model and usage figures are labelled as simulated; the workflow engine behind them is real code.
STATUS
Live. Read the case study, or open the platform itself.
